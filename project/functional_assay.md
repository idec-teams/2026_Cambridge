£ Conceptualization  
The functional assay wanted to directly measure activity of host protein fusions to quantify impacts in enzyme performance by our tags. This would have been done by quantifying a reactant or product over time and obtaining enzyme kinetics data.

Two proteins were identified for this purpose: taCA and isPETase.

£ taCA Functional Assay  
Initially, a colorimetric acid of the rate of change of pH was to be done to quantify activity of taCA. However, due to technical difficulties, an alternative method was planned to be used.

taCA also functions as an esterase. Colorimetric quantification of (chemical) would have been used instead.

£ isPETase Functional Assay  
isPETase also displays esterase activity. Its enzyme kinetics was also going to be done via colorimetric quantification of (chemical).
