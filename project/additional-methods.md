This section provides more details on the methodology used in the project alongside all troubleshooting experiments and rationale involved in experimental design.


### [Carbenicillin Selection System](/project/carb_selection.md)
This method was developed for high throughput wet lab screening of beneficial variants for protein refinement via directed evolution.

This method can also be used in validation of monoclonal variants when compared against the original variant.

### [Validation Assays](/project/functional_assay.md)
Validation assays are used in order to benchmark the performance of our proteins to confirm the direction of evolution. The goal is to show that the tags improve solubility, without impeding function, and are generalizable.

The secondary function is to benchmark how optimal our tags are to the evolutionary peak. This would have been done by first compacting the final sequences, compacting them into a degenerate sequence, and benchmarking the variants generated. This checks the sequence space around our winner sequences which will inform whether our method optimizes the tag to the evolutionary peak or whether they have room for evolution.

## [Solubility Assay](/project/solubility_assay.md)
This method was developed for a direct proxy for the solubility improvement conferred by our tags.

## Functional Assay
This method was developed as a validation that our tags do not impede function of the host protein.
